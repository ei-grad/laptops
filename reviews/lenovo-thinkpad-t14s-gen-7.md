# Lenovo ThinkPad T14s Gen 7

**Status:** Announced MWC 2026. Intel Panther Lake variant shipping. AMD Gorgon Point variant **later in 2026**.

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Weight | **1.1 kg** (lightest T-series ever) |
| Battery | 58 Wh |
| Display | 14" up to 1800p OLED (VRR 30-120Hz, 500 nits) |
| RAM | Soldered (not upgradeable) |

## Assessment

Weight reduction from 1.3 kg (Gen 6) to 1.1 kg is impressive but may compromise sustained thermals. AMD variant not yet available — wait for reviews.
